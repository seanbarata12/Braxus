Signs your water heater is failing

  • Water pooling around the base of the tank
  • Hot water running out faster than it used to
  • Rusty or discoloured hot water
  • Popping or rumbling sounds from the tank
  • The tank is more than 10 to 12 years old

Rental water heaters

Many GTA homes have a rented water heater. If you'd rather own your heater, we can install one you buy. Check your rental agreement first for any buyout or return terms, since those are between you and the rental company.

Water heaters FAQ

Something else? Call or text (647) 468-9696.

How long does it take to replace a water heater?

Most straight replacements take a few hours. Switching to tankless takes longer because of new venting and gas or electrical work.

Should I get a tankless water heater?

Tankless gives you endless hot water and saves space, but costs more to install. A tank is cheaper upfront. We'll talk through which fits your home and how you use hot water.

My water heater is leaking. What should I do?

Turn off the cold water supply valve on top of the tank and turn off its power or gas, then call or text us at (647) 468-9696.
