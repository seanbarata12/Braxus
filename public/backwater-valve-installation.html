Backwater valve installation in Toronto & the GTA

A backwater valve stops city sewer water from flowing back into your basement during heavy rain. We install it on your main sewer line, pull the permit, book the City inspection and help you claim the City of Toronto rebate.

Do you need one?

  • Your basement has flooded from a floor drain, toilet or shower during a storm
  • Your street or neighbourhood has a history of sewer backups
  • You're finishing the basement and want to protect the investment
  • Your insurer asked for one, or gives a discount for having one

The City of Toronto rebate

Toronto's Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy Program pays 80% of the invoiced cost of a backwater valve, up to $1,600 per valve (up to two valves) for work completed on or after November 12, 2025. The program also covers sump pumps (up to $2,250), sump pump battery backups ($300), weeping tile disconnection and capping ($400) and a home plumbing assessment ($500), for a maximum of $6,650 per property.

To qualify, the valve needs a building permit and a passed City inspection, and your invoice must be itemized and marked paid in full. You have two years after the work is done to apply. We set the job up so all of that is covered. See the City of Toronto program page for the full rules.

What affects the price

  • How deep the sewer line is under your floor
  • Floor finishes in the way (tile, carpet, built-in walls)
  • Whether a second sewer connection needs its own valve
  • Adding a sump pump or weeping tile disconnection at the same time

Backwater valves FAQ

Something else? Call or text (647) 468-9696.

How much does a backwater valve cost in Toronto?

It depends on how deep the sewer line is and what's on top of the floor. We give a free written quote after seeing the basement. With the City of Toronto subsidy covering 80% of the cost up to $1,600 per valve, many homeowners get a large share of it back.

Do I need a permit for a backwater valve?

Yes. In Toronto a backwater valve needs a building permit and a City inspection before the floor is closed. Both are also required for the rebate. We arrange them for you.

How long does installation take?

Most installs are done in one day, plus the City inspection visit. Your water and drains are only off for part of that day.

Does a backwater valve need maintenance?

Yes. Check and clean it about once a year, and after any major storm. We show you how when we install it, or we can do it for you.

Do you install backwater valves outside Toronto?

Yes. We install them across the GTA, including Markham, Richmond Hill, Vaughan, Pickering and Durham. Rebates outside Toronto depend on your municipality, so ask us and we'll tell you what we know.
