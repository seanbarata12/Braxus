When it keeps coming back

A drain that backs up again a few weeks after it's cleared usually has a bigger problem: roots, a sag, or a cracked or collapsed pipe. We can run a camera through the line to see what's going on. If the pipe needs replacing, see our sewer and drain line replacement page.

Drain backups & clogs FAQ

Something else? Call or text (647) 468-9696.

Why is water coming up through my basement floor drain?

Your main sewer line is blocked, or the City sewer is overloaded during heavy rain. If it only happens in storms, a backwater valve is the long-term fix. If it happens any time, the line is likely blocked or broken.

Do you clear main sewer lines?

Yes. We clear main line blockages and can camera the line afterwards to check for roots or damage.

Do you do commercial drain backups?

Yes. We clear drains for offices, retail units and restaurants across the GTA.

How can I stop my drains from clogging?

Keep grease, wipes and food scraps out of drains. If a drain keeps clogging anyway, have the line inspected, since roots and sagging pipe won't go away with cleaning.
