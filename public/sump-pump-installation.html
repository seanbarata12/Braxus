Sump pump installation & replacement

A working sump pump keeps groundwater out of your basement. We install new sump pits and pumps, replace pumps that have failed, and add high-water alarms and battery backups so you're protected when the power goes out.

What's included

  • New sump pit and basin, or replacement pump in your existing pit
  • Pump with high-water alarm, check valve and discharge piping
  • Discharge to the outside, away from the foundation (not into the sanitary sewer)
  • Battery backup pump, or a second pump for homes that take on a lot of water
  • Connecting weeping tile to the sump pit when it's being disconnected from the sewer

Signs it's time

  • The pump runs constantly, cycles on and off, or won't turn on
  • Loud grinding or rattling from the pit
  • Water around the pit or along the basement walls after rain
  • The pump is more than 7 to 10 years old
  • You've lost power during a storm and the basement took on water

Toronto rebate for sump pumps

The City of Toronto's Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy pays 80% of the cost of a sump pump with alarm, up to $2,250, plus up to $300 for a battery backup, for work completed on or after November 12, 2025. The battery backup must be listed separately on the invoice, and you'll need photos of the pump and its outside discharge pipe. We take care of the invoice and photos for you.

Sump pumps FAQ

Something else? Call or text (647) 468-9696.

How much does it cost to replace a sump pump?

Swapping a pump in an existing pit costs much less than installing a new pit. We give a free quote with the options listed so you can choose. In Toronto, the City subsidy can cover up to $2,250 of a sump pump with alarm.

Do I need a battery backup sump pump?

If your pump runs often, the backup is worth it. Storms that bring heavy rain also knock out power, and that's when the main pump stops. Toronto's subsidy covers up to $300 of a battery backup.

Can my sump pump drain into the sewer?

No. In Toronto and most GTA cities the sump pump must discharge outside, onto your property, not into the sanitary sewer.

How long does a sump pump last?

Usually 7 to 10 years. Test it a few times a year by pouring water into the pit until the pump turns on.
