  • Burst and leaking pipes

  • No hot water or a leaking water heater
  • Leaking or seized main shut-off valves
  • Frozen pipes

While you wait for us

  • Turn off the water at the main shut-off, usually in the basement near the water meter
  • For a leaking water heater, also turn off its cold water supply and its power or gas
  • Turn off power to any area where water is near outlets or lights
  • Move valuables out of the way and take photos for insurance

Commercial emergencies

For offices, retail units, restaurants and buildings, we handle burst supply lines, flooding, drain backups and washroom failures, and we isolate the problem so the rest of the building can keep running.

Emergency plumbing FAQ

Something else? Call or text (647) 468-9696.

Where is my main water shut-off?

In most GTA homes it's in the basement or utility room, on the front wall where the water line comes in, beside the water meter. Turn it clockwise to shut off water to the whole house.

What areas do you cover for emergencies?

We're based in Markham and cover Toronto, North York, Scarborough, Richmond Hill, Vaughan, Thornhill, Unionville, Stouffville, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby, Oshawa, Newmarket, Mississauga and Brampton.
