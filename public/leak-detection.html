Leaks we find and fix

  • Water coming through a ceiling from a bathroom or laundry room above
  • Leaking drain joints and supply lines inside walls
  • Shower and tub leaks
  • Leaks under the basement floor
  • Leaking main shut-off valves and water lines

Signs of a hidden leak

  • Stains or bubbling paint on a ceiling or wall
  • A musty smell or mould in one spot
  • The water meter moving when everything is off
  • A water bill that's higher than usual

Leak investigation FAQ

Something else? Call or text (647) 468-9696.

Why is water leaking through my ceiling?

Most often it's a shower, tub, toilet or drain joint in the room above, or a laundry or dishwasher drain. We test each one to find the source.

Do I need to open the wall or ceiling?

Sometimes. We start with testing to narrow it down, so that if an opening is needed it's small and in the right spot.

Can you give me a report for insurance?

Yes. We can write up what we found, the cause and photos, which most insurers ask for.

How do I check for a hidden leak?

Turn off every tap and appliance and watch the water meter. If it keeps moving, water is going somewhere. Call us and we'll find it.
