What we handle

Residential and commercial plumbing, including specialty valve service. Tap a category for details.

Residential Residential Emergency Plumbing Burst pipe, no hot water, or a backed-up drain — problems that can't wait for a scheduled visit.
  • Burst and leaking pipes
  • No hot water / water heater failure
  • Sewer and drain backups
  • Leaking or seized shut-off valves
  • Frozen pipe thawing
Emergency · Residential & Commercial Emergency Drain Backups Water or sewage coming up through a floor drain, toilet or tub? Call or text and we'll come out fast, clear the blockage and get your drains flowing again.
  • Basement floor drain backups
  • Main sewer line blockages
  • Toilets, tubs and showers backing up
  • Kitchen, laundry and sink drain clogs
  • Backwater valve checks after a backup
Residential Residential Renovations & Installations Bathroom and kitchen plumbing for renovations, additions, and fixture upgrades.
  • Bathroom & kitchen rough-in and reno plumbing
  • Fixture, faucet, and toilet installation
  • Water heater installation (tank & tankless)
  • Sump pump and backwater valve installation
  • Repiping and shut-off valve upgrades
Commercial Commercial Emergency Plumbing Fast response for offices, retail, and food-service properties when a plumbing failure puts the business on hold.
  • Burst supply lines and flooding response
  • Drain and sewer backups
  • Washroom and fixture failures
  • Emergency water shut-off and isolation
Commercial Commercial Renovations & Installations Plumbing for tenant fit-outs, office and retail renovations, restaurants, and new fixture or equipment installs.
  • Tenant fit-out and renovation rough-in
  • Commercial washroom and fixture installation
  • Commercial water heater installation
  • Grease interceptor and backflow preventer installation
  • Kitchen and equipment hookups for food service
Commercial Commercial Maintenance Scheduled inspections and preventive maintenance to keep a property's plumbing out of emergency territory.
  • Routine fixture and line inspections
  • Preventive maintenance scheduling
  • Backflow and valve checks
  • Tenant fixture repairs and upkeep
Specialty Maintenance PRV (Pressure Reducing Valve) Maintenance Keeping incoming water pressure in a safe range to protect pipes, fixtures, and appliances.
  • Pressure testing and diagnosis
  • PRV adjustment and calibration
  • PRV replacement
  • High-pressure damage prevention checks
Specialty Maintenance Mixing Valve Maintenance Thermostatic and tempering valve service to keep hot water at a safe, consistent temperature.
  • Mixing / tempering valve calibration
  • Temperature testing
  • Valve repair and replacement
  • Scald-protection compliance checks
