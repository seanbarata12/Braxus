What is a pressure reducing valve, and why does it need servicing?

A pressure reducing valve (PRV) lowers the water pressure coming in from the city main to a safe level for your plumbing. As it wears, pressure can creep up and strain pipes, fixtures, water heaters and appliances. Banging pipes, running toilets and dripping taps can be signs it needs a check.

Where is my main water shut-off valve?

In most GTA homes it's in the basement or utility room, on the wall where the water line comes in, close to the water meter. Turning it clockwise shuts off water to the whole house. If you can't find it or it won't turn, call us.
