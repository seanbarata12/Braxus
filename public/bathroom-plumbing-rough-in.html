Bathroom & reno plumbing FAQ

How much does a basement bathroom rough-in cost?

It depends on how far the fixtures are from the main drain and how much floor has to be broken. We give a free written quote after seeing the space.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom rough-in?

Most new plumbing, like a new bathroom, needs a building permit and inspection in Ontario. Replacing fixtures in the same spot usually doesn't. We'll tell you what your project needs.

Do you work with general contractors?

Yes. A good share of our rough-in work is for contractors and renovation companies.
